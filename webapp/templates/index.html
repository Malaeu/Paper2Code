How It Works

1
Upload Paper and Dataset

Start by uploading your scientific paper (PDF, JSON, or markdown) and your dataset (CSV, Parquet, Excel, or JSON).

2
Configure Adaptation

Our system analyzes your dataset and proposes variable mappings. You can review and adjust these mappings if needed.

3
Review Adaptation Plan

We generate a detailed adaptation plan that explains how the paper's methodology will be adapted to your dataset. You can review this plan before proceeding.

4
Get Your Code

After approving the plan, our system generates a complete code repository implementing the paper's methodology with your dataset's variables.
